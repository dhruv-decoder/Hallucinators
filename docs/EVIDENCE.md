## 3. Measured detector quality

### 3a. Synthetic seed — 🔁 `make eval`
Per-axis P/R/F1/FPR/FNR vs no-oversight and flag-everything baselines, plus ECE, on a clearly-labelled
synthetic seed set (18 examples). Wiring numbers, not a benchmark.

### 3b. Real public benchmark (HaluEval QA) — ✅ measured, reproducible
`python -m controlplane.eval.run_real --dataset halueval --limit N [--models]` (needs `pip install
datasets`; `--models` needs the `[ml]` extra). Measured Aug 2026 on the groundedness/performance axis:

| Detector stack | P | R | F1 | FPR | FNR | cleared@T0 | added latency |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lexical heuristic (T0 only), n=200 | 0.73 | 0.19 | **0.30** | 0.07 | 0.81 | 100% | 6 ms |
| VoI cascade → **HHEM-2.1** on the tail, n=100 | 0.89 | 0.66 | **0.76** | 0.08 | 0.34 | 53% | 142 ms* |
| baseline: flag-everything | 0.50 | 1.00 | 0.67 | 1.00 | 0.00 | — | — |

*added latency is incurred only on the ~47% of requests the VoI rule judged uncertain enough to climb to
HHEM; the 53% cleared at T0 pay ~0 ms. **Honest reading:** the cheap lexical check is genuinely poor on real
hallucinations (recall 0.19 — they reuse the source's words), which is exactly why the cascade must be able to
buy a model on the tail. HHEM lifts F1 0.30→0.76 and beats flag-everything, while staying cheap on the
majority path. This is the whole VoI thesis, on real data.

> Environment bug found & fixed: HHEM-2.1-Open's `trust_remote_code` model loads with **random weights on
> `transformers` 5.x** (silent — only a "newly initialized" notice), giving F1≈0.29. Pinned `transformers<5`
> (verified on 4.44.2). Numbers above are with the correct pin.

**Still to do (P3):** RAGTruth (loader shipped), a license-checked PII/red-team set, and larger-n runs on the
A100 for tight confidence intervals.

### 3c. Latency / throughput — ✅ measured (`POST /v1/oversight/jobs/benchmark`)
Local cascade (T2 judge excluded — it fires only on the tail), N=1,500 on a laptop:
**added latency p50 0.12 ms / p95 0.16 ms / p99 0.33 ms**, **~7,100 req/s**, 100% cleared at T0. At 50k
interactions/week this extrapolates to a net-negative P&L (savings > safety spend) — labelled an extrapolation
from simulated traffic at the sourced prices above, not production billing.
